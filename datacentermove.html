--- layout: default title: Datacenter Move description: BIL Datacenter Move October 2026. ---

10-23-2026 Update: The recabling and reassembly of equipment at the new datacenter is going slower than originally anticipated. Downtime related to the move is expected to continue into the early part of the week of October 25.

The BIL infrastructure will be migrated to a new data center in October. Metadata services through the api.brainimagelibrary.org domain will remain accessable during the move.

Following the physical relocation, BIL systems will be restored in phases.

Bridges-2 will remain available throughout the BIL migration but the BIL filesystem will be unavailable on all systems during the migration window.

We appreciate your patience and understanding as we complete these infrastructure upgrades, which will provide a more robust and resilient computing environment for the BIL community.

If you require assistance, please send e-mail to bil-support@psc.edu.
