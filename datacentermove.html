--- layout: default title: Datacenter Move description: BIL Datacenter Move October 15-16, 2026. ---

The BIL infrastructure will be migrated to a new data center between October 5 and October 16.

Following the physical relocation, BIL systems will be restored in phases. We currently expect all BIL services to be fully available no later than October 16, with many services anticipated to return sooner.

Bridges-2 will remain available throughout the BIL migration but the BIL filesystem will be unavailable on all systems during the migration window.

We appreciate your patience and understanding as we complete these infrastructure upgrades, which will provide a more robust and resilient computing environment for the BIL community.

If you require assistance, please send e-mail to bil-support@psc.edu.
