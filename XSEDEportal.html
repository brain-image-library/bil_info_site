Create an XSEDE Portal Account

Overview

An XSEDE portal account is required to submit data to the Brain Image Library. The steps to create an XSEDE Portal Account are:
  1. Visit the XSEDE Portal Website: portal.xsede.org
  2. Enter the Required Information
  3. Verify your XSEDE Portal Account

How to Create an XSEDE Portal Account

  1. Visit the XSEDE Portal Website: portal.xsede.org To get an XSEDE portal ID, visit portal.xsede.org and click on the blue Create Account button on the left hand side of the screen. (See circle shown in red in the image below).

  2. Enter the Required Information Fill in all of the personal information requested. You will also need to choose a passkey. The passkey is just a string of text that you make up. You will use this passkey in the next step to finish creating your account. Upon submission, the system will send you a verification code in email that you will also need to use in the next step.

  3. Verify your XSEDE Portal Account Verify your account using your email address, the passkey that you selected in the previous step, and the verification code sent to you via email. Also, you will need to choose a username and a password.
